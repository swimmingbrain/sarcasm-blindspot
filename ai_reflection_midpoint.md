## Our Experience with GenAI

During Phase I, we mainly used ChatGPT to help us with coding, finding a suitable model, creating visualizations, and understanding our results.

At first, we planned to use a sentiment classifier to investigate sarcasm. After looking more closely at the available models, we realized that an irony detection model would fit our research question better, so we decided to switch.

AI was especially useful for working with the TweetEval dataset, implementing the experiments, and generating plots to compare how removing hashtags and emojis affects the model's predictions.

However, not everything AI suggested worked as expected. Some code needed adjustments, and the first visualizations were too complicated or did not clearly show what we wanted. We had to give additional instructions and improve the results ourselves.

We also noticed that AI sometimes suggested solutions without fully considering our specific task. Therefore, we had to check its suggestions instead of just accepting them.

Overall, GenAI saved us quite some time, especially with coding and debugging. We learned that it is a useful tool for getting started and solving problems, but we still need to understand the code, verify the results, and make the final decisions ourselves.
